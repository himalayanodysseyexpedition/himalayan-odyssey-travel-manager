Choose a route that matches the season

  • The Shimla–Kinnaur approach through Narkanda, Kalpa, Nako and Tabo is a common route into Spiti.
  • The Manali–Kunzum–Kaza route is seasonal. Plan it only when authorities confirm that the pass and road are open.
  • Snow, landslides, road work and local restrictions can change the route. Keep buffer time and check official updates before departure.

What a Spiti itinerary can include

  • Kaza and nearby villages, monasteries and high-altitude landscapes, depending on local access and opening conditions.
  • Kinnaur stopovers such as Kalpa or Sangla on a route via Shimla, depending on trip duration.
  • Time for acclimatisation, rest and weather buffers rather than trying to cover every stop in a rushed schedule.

7 nights / 8 days Spiti Valley package itinerary

This is a planning framework, not a fixed departure promise. The final overnight stops and sightseeing order depend on road conditions, season, accommodation and your starting point.

  1. Day 1: Chandigarh to Narkanda for the first overnight halt.
  2. Day 2: Narkanda to Sangla in the Baspa Valley.
  3. Day 3: Sangla to Tabo via Khab and Nako; Chitkul only if access and safe driving time permit.
  4. Day 4: Tabo to Kaza, with time for rest and acclimatisation.
  5. Day 5: Kaza local sightseeing, with Key Monastery and nearby villages selected according to access, daylight and traveller health.
  6. Day 6: Kaza to Kalpa for an overnight halt in Kinnaur.
  7. Day 7: Kalpa to Shimla for the final overnight stay.
  8. Day 8: Return to Chandigarh or the agreed drop point.

What to compare before choosing a Spiti package

  • Check whether the quote starts from Chandigarh, Shimla or another point, and whether the return drop is included.
  • Compare vehicle type, accommodation category, meal plan, room sharing and any local sightseeing vehicle separately.
  • Ask how the itinerary handles acclimatisation, weather delays, road closures and changes to village access.
  • Get inclusions, exclusions, cancellation terms and payment schedule in writing. A low headline price is not comparable if transport or meals are excluded.

Spiti Valley package cost and inclusions

The cost of a Spiti Valley tour package depends on the number of travellers, starting point, trip duration, vehicle category, room occupancy, meal plan and season. A shared group seat and a private vehicle quotation are not directly comparable.

  • Ask whether transport is shared or private and whether local sightseeing is included.
  • Check the number of hotel or homestay nights, room-sharing basis and meal plan.
  • Confirm fuel, driver allowance, tolls, parking, permits where applicable, taxes and entry fees.
  • Ask what happens if road closures, weather or local restrictions require an extra night or route change.
  • Request a written day-wise itinerary with inclusions and exclusions before paying an advance.

Frequently asked questions

How many days are needed for a Spiti Valley tour?

The duration depends on your starting point, route, season and the time needed for acclimatisation and local sightseeing. Share your dates for a practical itinerary.

Can I visit Spiti from Manali?

The Manali–Kunzum–Kaza route is seasonal and can remain closed in winter. Confirm current official road status before planning a circuit that uses it.

Do you offer group and private Spiti tours?

You can enquire about group departures and customised private trips. Dates, vehicle, accommodation and availability are confirmed for each enquiry.

How much does a Spiti Valley tour package cost?

There is no single fixed price for every trip. The quotation depends on dates, group size, pickup point, vehicle, accommodation, meal plan and duration. Request an itemised quote for your exact travel plan.

Can I book a private Spiti Valley tour?

You can request a customised private trip. The vehicle, route, overnight halts, accommodation and final price are confirmed after reviewing your dates and group size.

What is a typical Spiti Valley tour duration?

A full circuit is often planned over about 7–9 days, while shorter trips cover fewer places. Choose a realistic itinerary with suitable overnight halts and buffer time for mountain-road conditions.
