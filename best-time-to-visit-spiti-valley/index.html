January and February: deep winter

Expect severe cold and potential road closures. Use the Shimla–Kinnaur approach only when authorities permit, confirm operating accommodation and heating, and avoid relying on the Manali route. See Winter Spiti tour enquiries.

March and April: late winter and spring

March may still bring winter conditions and restricted village access. April can be transitional, with variable snow and cold nights. For a festival trip, check our Sangla Holi and Spiti guide; confirm local dates and the road route.

May and June: early summer

The Kinnaur approach may be more practical for early summer. Snow clearing and pass access vary year by year, so do not assume Chandratal or Kunzum will be open. Request summer Spiti tour options that can adapt to access.

July and August: summer and monsoon travel

Spiti is relatively dry compared with much of Himachal, but the approach roads can experience heavy rain, landslides and closures. A summer circuit may be possible when officially open. Check forecasts and keep extra time for road delays.

September and October: autumn travel

September can be a useful period for a summer-style itinerary if routes remain open. In October, nights get colder and seasonal camps and roads may close. Confirm the Manali exit and Chandratal separately rather than relying on an old itinerary.

November and December: return of winter

Expect colder conditions and fewer seasonal accommodation options. Plan around the Kinnaur approach, with flexibility for road closures. Christmas and New Year dates are subject to separately announced departures. Snowfall during any particular trip is not guaranteed.

Choose a month for your travel style

For a first visit, the warmer months generally offer more options, but road conditions and altitude still matter. For snow landscapes, a winter trip requires more preparation and flexibility. Compare winter versus summer Spiti and sightseeing options before selecting dates.

Frequently asked questions

Which month is best for Spiti Valley?

The best month depends on the route and experience you want. Warmer months generally offer more options; winter needs specific preparation and route checks.

Is Spiti safe during monsoon months?

No season is risk-free. Approach roads can be affected by rainfall and landslides even though Spiti itself is relatively dry. Check current advisories.

When does Chandratal Lake open in 2027?

No fixed opening date is guaranteed. Check official road status and authorised camp access before booking.
