About Himalayan Odyssey Expedition

Himalayan Odyssey Expedition is a travel operator based in Narkanda, Himachal Pradesh, India. We arrange group departures, private trips and customised journeys across Spiti Valley, Kinnaur and other Himalayan destinations.

Plan your Himalayan journey with a local operator

Our Narkanda base is on the Shimla–Kinnaur approach to Spiti. We help travellers select routes, overnight stops, transport and accommodation based on their travel dates, group size and preferences. Mountain routes and excursions are subject to weather, current road conditions and local permissions.

Our travel services

  • Spiti Valley group and private tour packages
  • Winter Spiti tours and group departures
  • Kinnaur tours covering Sangla, Chitkul and Kalpa
  • Spiti tours from Chandigarh
  • Narkanda stays, cabs and local trips
  • B2B ground services for travel agencies

How to enquire and book

Share your dates, starting city, number of travellers, preferred destinations and accommodation requirements. Ask for a written itinerary, confirmed inclusions, exclusions, price, payment terms and arrangements for changes before paying an advance. Read our travel guides for practical route preparation.
