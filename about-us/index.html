A Himalayan road journey is more than a list of stops. The season, road access, travel time, accommodation and pace all shape the experience. Our role is to help you discuss those practical details before you decide on a trip.

A Narkanda base for Himalayan travel

Our office is in Narkanda, a mountain town in Himachal Pradesh. From this base, we help travellers plan journeys across the Shimla–Kinnaur side and onward toward Spiti when route conditions permit. We also help guests explore other Himachal destinations, including Manali.

Clear details before you book

Before confirming a journey, ask us to clarify the day-wise itinerary, pickup and drop points, vehicle, accommodation, meal plan, inclusions, exclusions, payment schedule and cancellation terms. For winter and high-altitude trips, route access and services can change, so we discuss current conditions and practical alternatives rather than treating an old itinerary as a guarantee.

How to start planning

Share your travel dates or month, pickup city, number of travellers, preferred destinations and approximate budget. We can then discuss available group departures or a private journey and confirm current prices and inclusions for your dates.
