May and June Spiti trips

The Kinnaur approach through Narkanda, Sangla, Nako and Tabo can be considered for early summer. Snow clearance on Kunzum Pass and the Chandratal access road may still be in progress. Never book an early summer trip assuming the full circuit is open.

July, August and September Spiti packages

Later summer may offer more circuit options, including a Manali exit and a seasonal Chandratal visit if officially accessible. Spiti lies in a relatively dry region, but roads through lower Himachal and Kinnaur can face monsoon rain, landslides and delays. Build in a buffer day and confirm official advisories.

Spiti short circuit or full circuit with Chandratal camping?

A shorter Manali-to-Kaza circuit may suit travellers with limited time only when the seasonal road is open and a gradual ascent and rest plan is feasible. A longer Shimla–Kinnaur–Spiti–Manali circuit can allow more overnight halts and sightseeing. Request a customised six- to ten-day itinerary; neither duration is an announced fixed departure.

Full Spiti Valley circuit: Shimla to Manali with Chandratal

Which Spiti Valley circuit should you book? For a seasonal full circuit from Chandigarh, compare the Shimla–Kinnaur–Tabo–Kaza–Kunzum–Manali route with a return through Kinnaur. The former offers a different return route only when the high passes are open; the latter is the more practical winter planning option. Ask for an itinerary showing overnight stops and return pickup before booking.

Unlike a Kinnaur-side round trip, a full summer circuit enters through Shimla and Kinnaur, spends time around Tabo and Kaza, then considers a seasonal exit via Kunzum Pass and Manali. A Chandratal stop is optional and depends on official road and camping access. Allow adequate time for acclimatisation, weather buffers and overnight halts rather than promising a fixed number of stops.

For a Manali-to-Spiti tour enquiry, confirm the actual joining point and gradual ascent plan before arranging transport. An Atal Tunnel crossing does not mean the entire route to Kaza is open. If Kunzum is closed, request a return route through Kinnaur. Compare our Chandratal trip guide and summer circuit cab enquiries.
