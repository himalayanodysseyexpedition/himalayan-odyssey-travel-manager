Cab and vehicle enquiries

When requesting a cab, share your pickup and drop locations, travel dates, group size, luggage needs and preferred vehicle type. Mountain routes may require suitable ground clearance, experienced local drivers and realistic driving times. Vehicle availability, route suitability and final rates are confirmed directly for each enquiry.

Hotels, homestays and properties

For accommodation, include your destination, check-in and check-out dates, number of guests, room configuration and meal preferences. Ask for the property location, facilities, room category, cancellation terms and final payable amount before confirming a stay.
