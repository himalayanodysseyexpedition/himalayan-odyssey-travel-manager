Choose a Kaza stay for your sightseeing itinerary

Kaza can serve as a base for trips to Key Monastery, Kibber, Chicham, Langza, Hikkim and Komic when accessible. Ask about travel time from the property to the main market, road access and parking. If you need transport, enquire about a Kaza local sightseeing taxi separately.

Budget hotels, homestays and private rooms

Solo guests can ask about single occupancy or shared accommodation where available. Couples and families should specify bed configuration, private bathroom and meal requirements. Larger groups need to confirm the total number of rooms in the same property rather than assuming all guests can stay together.

Kaza hotels in winter: room heating and water

Confirm that the property is open, how rooms are heated, whether electric blankets are supplied and what hot-water arrangements work in freezing weather. Ask about backup plans during power interruptions and safe heating practices. Winter operations differ from summer.

Kaza room prices and booking terms

Request the nightly room rate, taxes, breakfast or dinner charges, extra bedding, advance payment and cancellation conditions. Compare total stay cost rather than only the headline room tariff. No live inventory or fixed rates are displayed here.

How many nights should you book in Kaza?

A stay of two or more nights can allow rest and local exploration on a multi-day itinerary, but the duration depends on your route and health. Do not rush high-altitude activities after arrival. If altitude symptoms worsen, descend and seek medical assistance.

Before paying for accommodation

Confirm the property's name and address, room type, dates, total price, payment terms and facilities in writing. Check official Spiti road updates before travelling towards Spiti. If travelling from another Indian city, consult our Pan-India joining guide.

Plan your stay around the places to visit near Kaza and allow time for rest and acclimatisation.

Frequently asked questions

Can I book a Kaza homestay for a family?

You can enquire for family rooms or multiple rooms, with property availability and facilities confirmed for your dates.

Do all Kaza hotels have heating?

No. Heating and electric blanket availability vary by property and season; ask for confirmation.

Can I arrange sightseeing from my Kaza hotel?

Yes, you can request a separate local taxi arrangement subject to road access, local rules and vehicle availability.
