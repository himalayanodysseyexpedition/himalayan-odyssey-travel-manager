Places to visit in Spiti Valley: sightseeing guide

Plan the places to visit in Spiti Valley around your overnight stops, road access and altitude acclimatisation. This guide groups the attractions into realistic sightseeing circuits instead of treating them as a single day trip.

Kaza and nearby villages

Kaza is a convenient base for exploring the central valley. Visit Key (Ki or Kye) Monastery, Kibber and Chicham on one possible circuit; consider Langza, Hikkim and Komic on another. Read our Kaza sightseeing and village guide for planning details.

Tabo, Dhankar and Gue

Tabo is known for its historic monastery, and Dhankar for its monastery and dramatic setting above the valley. Gue (Giu) village is known for its preserved mummy. These stops belong to the eastern Spiti approach; allow adequate travel time and confirm opening hours and access before visiting.

Pin Valley and Mud village

Pin Valley branches off the main Spiti valley and offers a quieter landscape of villages and mountains. A Pin Valley day trip or overnight visit should be planned separately if you want time for local exploration.

Chandratal Lake and Kunzum Pass

Chandratal is an alpine lake near the seasonal Manali–Kaza approach. It is not a dependable winter attraction. Read our Chandratal Lake trip guide for access, camping and itinerary considerations.

Spiti Valley altitude: how high are Tabo, Kaza and Kunzum Pass?

Spiti is a high-altitude valley, not a destination with one single elevation. According to Himachal Tourism and the district administration, Tabo is about 3,049 metres (10,004 ft), Kaza 3,660 metres, and Dhankar 3,870 metres. The district's official overview puts Kunzum Pass at 4,551 metres.

Plan a gradual ascent, rest periods and flexible sightseeing. Do not ascend further if altitude illness symptoms develop; seek medical advice and descend if symptoms worsen. For a slower approach through Kinnaur and Tabo, compare the eight-day itinerary and Spiti group and private tour packages. High village excursions and seasonal passes require separate access checks.

Spiti sightseeing itinerary and trip duration

On a typical multi-day Kinnaur approach, allow time for Tabo and central Spiti, with at least a rest period in Kaza before high villages. Avoid combining distant attractions just to finish a checklist. See the eight-day Spiti itinerary for a route example and winter tour guidance for seasonal alternatives.

Frequently asked questions

What are the main places to visit in Spiti Valley?

Kaza, Key Monastery, Kibber, Chicham, Langza, Hikkim, Komic, Tabo, Dhankar and Pin Valley are popular planning options. Chandratal is seasonal.

Can I cover all Spiti sightseeing in one day?

No. The attractions span different parts of the valley and high mountain roads. Split them into suitable circuits and allow rest.

Which sightseeing places can I visit in winter?

Access depends on snow, road status and local restrictions. The Kinnaur approach and selected central valley stops may be possible, but confirm each visit locally.
