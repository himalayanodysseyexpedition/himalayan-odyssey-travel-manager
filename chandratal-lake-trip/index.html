Chandratal Lake trip: seasonal access, camping and route planning

Chandratal, also written Chandra Taal or Chandrataal, is a high-altitude lake near Kunzum Pass. Treat it as a seasonal extension of a Spiti itinerary, not a guaranteed stop on every departure.

Where is Chandratal Lake?

The lake lies in the high mountain landscape near the Kunzum route between Lahaul and Spiti. The approach branches from the seasonal road near Batal. Travel involves rough mountain sections and a final approach that may require walking; confirm the current access and parking arrangements.

Best time to plan Chandratal

The warmer travel season is generally the relevant period, often around summer to early autumn when the access roads and camp operations permit. The opening date varies each year. Check official road updates for your dates rather than relying on a fixed month or an old social media post.

Chandratal camping and overnight stays

Ask about authorised camps at permitted sites away from the sensitive lakeshore. Do not pitch a tent beside the lake or assume camps are operating. Confirm bedding, toilets, meals, heating arrangements, cancellation terms and vehicle pickup. Pack out rubbish and follow conservation rules.

Kaza to Chandratal to Manali or a return via Kinnaur

A summer circuit may include Chandratal between Kaza and Manali when Kunzum and the onward roads are officially accessible. If the road is closed, return through the Kinnaur approach instead. Leave sufficient buffer for weather and avoid tight onward flights or trains.

Altitude, weather and safety

Chandratal is at high altitude with cold nights and limited services. Acclimatise gradually, keep warm and avoid strenuous activity if unwell. If symptoms of altitude illness worsen, descend and seek medical help. Do not enter the lake or walk on ice.

Frequently asked questions

Can I visit Chandratal Lake in December or January?

Do not plan a regular road visit in deep winter. The seasonal approach usually closes and access must be confirmed with the authorities.

Can I camp next to Chandratal Lake?

Use only authorised designated camping sites and follow local conservation restrictions. Do not camp on the lakeshore.

Is Chandratal included in every Spiti tour?

No. It is a seasonal extension that depends on official road access, the route, weather and confirmed inclusions.
