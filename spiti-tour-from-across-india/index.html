How to join a Spiti Valley trip from your city

Himalayan Odyssey Expedition is a tour operator based in Narkanda, Himachal Pradesh. Travellers from across India can enquire about our Spiti Valley tour packages. Chandigarh is a common joining point; Delhi pickup or other transfer arrangements must be agreed for the selected trip. Flights and trains from your home city are not included unless expressly stated in your quote.

First confirm your departure date and joining point, then arrange your connecting journey. Compare our Chandigarh Spiti packages and Delhi to Spiti travel guide. Check current schedules with the transport provider; the Chandigarh airport passenger information and Chandigarh Tourism transport guide are useful starting points.

Plan your Spiti Valley trip from Delhi, Gujarat, West Bengal or Chandigarh

If you are travelling from Ahmedabad, Surat, Kolkata, Delhi or another Indian city, first confirm your group departure's actual reporting point and time. Chandigarh and Ambala may be joining points for selected tours; direct pickup from your home city is not automatically included. Compare the Chandigarh package options, Delhi route guide and seasonal Spiti Valley circuit. Travel connections by state and city

Travel connections by state and city

Maharashtra: Mumbai, Pune, Nagpur, Nashik

For a Mumbai or Pune to Spiti Valley trip, compare flights to Chandigarh or Delhi and rail connections. If arriving from Nagpur or Nashik, consider a connection through a larger airport if it suits the confirmed departure. Keep the arrival day separate from the mountain itinerary when needed.

Gujarat: Ahmedabad, Surat, Vadodara, Rajkot

Compare Ahmedabad to Spiti Valley tour options by first checking flights or trains towards the confirmed joining city. From Surat, Vadodara and Rajkot, compare local connections as well as travel through Ahmedabad or Delhi; do not book a non-refundable onward transfer until the tour timing is confirmed.

Karnataka: Bangalore (Bengaluru), Mysore, Mangalore

For a Bangalore to Spiti Valley package, compare Chandigarh and Delhi airport connections. Travellers from Mysore or Mangalore may need an additional transfer; include that time in the overall holiday rather than in the advertised Spiti road-trip duration.

West Bengal: Kolkata, Siliguri, Durgapur

For a Kolkata to Spiti Valley tour, compare flight and rail options to the agreed northern joining point. Siliguri travellers can compare Bagdogra airport connections; Durgapur travellers can compare local connections or Kolkata depending on their dates.

Rajasthan: Jaipur, Udaipur, Jodhpur, Kota, Ajmer, Bikaner

For a Jaipur to Spiti Valley trip, compare rail, road and air options to Chandigarh or Delhi. From Udaipur, Jodhpur and other Rajasthan cities, compare the total transfer time rather than the headline tour duration.

Uttarakhand: Dehradun, Haridwar, Rishikesh, Haldwani

Compare road or rail connections to the agreed pickup point. A journey from Uttarakhand does not necessarily require travel through Delhi; ask whether an alternative joining point is possible for your private trip.

Southern India: Hyderabad, Chennai, Coimbatore, Kochi, Thiruvananthapuram, Kozhikode, Visakhapatnam, Vijayawada

Compare flight connections to Chandigarh and Delhi. If a connecting flight or long train journey is required, plan additional days outside the Spiti itinerary and avoid a tight return connection.

Central and northern India: Indore, Bhopal, Jabalpur, Lucknow, Noida, Agra, Kanpur, Varanasi, Meerut

Compare rail, road and air connections based on your origin city. For travellers close to Delhi, ask about the agreed meeting point and whether onward travel to Chandigarh is needed.

Punjab and Haryana: Ludhiana, Amritsar, Jalandhar, Patiala, Gurugram, Faridabad, Ambala, Karnal, Panipat

Road or rail connections to Chandigarh may be practical. Ask for exact reporting time and pickup location, especially when arranging a same-day connection.

Eastern, northeastern and other regions: Bhubaneswar, Cuttack, Patna, Gaya, Ranchi, Jamshedpur, Guwahati, Raipur, Bilaspur, Panaji, Margao

Compare the most practical local airport or rail connection, allowing time for transfers. For longer journeys, reserve a buffer night before departure and after the tour when necessary.

Book a group tour, private cab, hotel or sightseeing

  • Group tour: Ask for available seats, departure dates, shared transport and accommodation.
  • Private cab or Tempo Traveller: Share pickup point, passenger count, luggage and dates. Ask for vehicle suitability, fuel, driver charges, tolls and parking in the quote.
  • Hotels and homestays: Ask about Kaza, Tabo, Sangla or Kalpa stays, room sharing and heating arrangements in winter.
  • Local sightseeing: Ask about Key Monastery, Hikkim, Komic, Langza, Kibber and Chicham, subject to road access.

For the mountain portion, compare the 8-day itinerary and trip cost guide. Winter travellers should also review winter Spiti departures. Keep time for gradual ascent and weather-related delays. Check official road status before departure.

Frequently asked questions

Frequently asked questions

Can I book a Spiti Valley tour from Mumbai or Bangalore?

Yes, you can enquire about a tour while living in any city. Confirm the actual joining point before booking your own flight or train; city-to-joining-point travel is not included unless specified in your written quotation.

Can I join a Spiti trip from Gujarat, Rajasthan or West Bengal?

Yes. Share your origin city and travel dates. We can discuss joining a group departure or arranging a private itinerary from the agreed pickup point.

Can I book only a taxi, hotels or local sightseeing?

You can request transport-only, accommodation or sightseeing assistance separately. Availability, vehicle suitability and inclusions must be confirmed for your dates.

Should I fly to Chandigarh or Delhi for Spiti?

Choose the airport or railway station only after confirming your tour joining point. Compare the onward connection and keep a buffer for delays. A Delhi arrival does not automatically mean Delhi pickup is included.
