HIMALAYAN ODYSSEY EXPEDITION
CHANDIGARH–KINNAUR–SPITI: 7 NIGHTS / 8 DAYS
Planning reference • 26 November 2026

Day 1: Chandigarh to Narkanda — overnight Narkanda.
Day 2: Narkanda to Sangla — overnight Sangla.
Day 3: Sangla to Tabo via Khab and Nako, if time and access permit — overnight Tabo.
Day 4: Tabo to Kaza — keep arrival day light; overnight Kaza.
Day 5: Kaza local sightseeing, according to weather, road access and health — overnight Kaza.
Day 6: Kaza to Kalpa — overnight Kalpa.
Day 7: Kalpa to Shimla — overnight Shimla.
Day 8: Shimla to Chandigarh — agreed drop point.

Seven nights: Narkanda, Sangla, Tabo, Kaza (two nights), Kalpa, Shimla.
This is a reference itinerary, not a confirmed departure or a completed trip report. Allow extra days for delays and gradual ascent. In winter use the Kinnaur approach only when safe and permitted. The Kunzum/Manali exit is seasonal.

Before booking: confirm dates, pickup/drop, vehicle, room sharing, heating, meals, inclusions, excluded charges and the plan for road delays. Check road and weather advisories. Seek medical advice for high-altitude travel if appropriate.
