What to compare in an itinerary

Check the number of nights, daily driving time, pickup and drop point, vehicle category, accommodation, room-sharing basis, meal plan, sightseeing transfers and exclusions. Mountain journeys can change because of weather, road closures or local restrictions, so keep buffer time and confirm the final route shortly before departure. For Spiti, avoid rushing high-altitude days and discuss acclimatisation before booking.

Choose a realistic pace for mountain travel

Distances on a Himalayan route can take longer than they appear on a map because of narrow roads, bends, weather and road works. When comparing two itineraries, look at the time spent driving each day as well as the number of places listed. A plan with fewer stops and enough time for meals, rest and acclimatisation can be more comfortable than a rushed schedule. Confirm which sightseeing points are included, which are optional and whether local transfers are part of the quote. For winter journeys, ask how the operator will communicate route changes and what alternatives may be available if a road is closed.
