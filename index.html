WINTER SPITI EXPEDITION

This Departure’s Itinerary

26 December 2026 – 02 January 2027 • 7 Nights / 8 Days

Pickup & Drop: Ambala and Chandigarh

Price: ₹17,000 per person

Day-wise itinerary

  1. Day 1 — Chandigarh to NarkandaPickup from Ambala and Chandigarh, followed by the drive to Narkanda. Overnight stay at Narkanda.
  2. Day 2 — Narkanda to SanglaTravel through the mountain roads to Sangla Valley. Overnight stay at Sangla.
  3. Day 3 — Sangla to TaboContinue the journey from Sangla to Tabo. Overnight stay at Tabo.
  4. Day 4 — Tabo to KazaDrive from Tabo to Kaza. Overnight stay at Kaza.
  5. Day 5 — Kaza (Local Stay)Stay another night in Kaza, allowing time to explore the surrounding area as per weather and road conditions. Overnight stay at Kaza.
  6. Day 6 — Kaza to KalpaTravel from Kaza to Kalpa. Overnight stay at Kalpa.
  7. Day 7 — Kalpa to ShimlaBegin the return journey towards Shimla. Overnight stay at Shimla.
  8. Day 8 — Shimla to Chandigarh DropDrive to Chandigarh for the final drop.

The itinerary sequence and travel timings may be adjusted by the tour team according to weather, road and safety conditions.
