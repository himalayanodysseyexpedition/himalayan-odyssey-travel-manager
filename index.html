BEFORE YOU TRAVEL

Frequently Asked Questions

A few useful starting points. Our team can confirm the current package details for your dates and group.

How do I enquire about a Spiti Valley tour?

Browse current packages or use Build Your Trip to combine cabs, stays and tour packages in one enquiry. Review your selections and send them to our team on WhatsApp.

Do you arrange group and private trips?

You can contact us about both group departures and private Himalayan road trips. Share your preferred dates and number of travellers so we can discuss the available options.

What is included in a tour package?

Inclusions vary by package. Please check the package details and ask our team to confirm transport, accommodation, meals, sightseeing and any exclusions for your selected trip before making a payment.

Can you help with hotels and local transport?

Yes, you can enquire separately about mountain stays and vehicles through the Hotels and Cabs sections. Availability, route suitability and rates should be confirmed with our team for your travel dates.

What should I share when planning my trip?

Share your destination, tentative travel dates or month, number of travellers, pickup preference and approximate budget. This helps our team suggest relevant options.

Are routes and travel plans affected by weather?

Mountain road and weather conditions can change. Routes, access and sightseeing plans may need adjustment; please confirm the latest conditions and practical itinerary with our team before departure.
