Where to stay in Spiti Valley

Kaza is a practical base for local sightseeing, while Tabo can be an overnight halt on a Kinnaur-side itinerary. Nako is a possible stop on the approach to Spiti. Select overnight stays around your actual driving days and altitude acclimatisation rather than booking every night in one town.

Choose accommodation by your overnight route

For a Kinnaur-side road trip, decide your overnight towns before booking rooms. Tabo accommodation can suit an approach halt; Kaza hotels and homestays suit multi-night local sightseeing. Compare Kinnaur stays for the approach and return legs. Confirm current check-in dates, room occupancy, bathroom type and meal plan for each property.

For winter, ask about heated rooms and backup arrangements using our winter stay checklist. Guests who also need transfers or a complete itinerary can compare Spiti tour, cab and accommodation services.

Hotels, homestays and guesthouses

For a budget trip, ask about basic guesthouse or homestay rooms, bathroom arrangements and meals. For families or private groups, specify room occupancy, attached bathrooms, accessibility needs and parking. A homestay may offer a more personal experience, but facilities vary by property.

Winter Spiti accommodation and heated rooms

Ask whether the property is operating on your dates, and confirm room heating, electric blankets, hot water and any shared heated dining area separately. Heating may depend on electricity and fuel availability. Never assume a summer listing provides the same facilities in winter.

How much do hotels in Spiti Valley cost?

Room rates vary by date, town, occupancy, meal plan, bathroom and heating requirements. Request a per-room, per-night quotation with taxes, meals, extra bed charges and cancellation terms. We do not advertise a fixed rate or confirm room inventory without checking.

Combine stays with transport

If you need a cab between hotels, share the overnight towns and dates when requesting a Spiti Valley taxi quote. Compare our complete Spiti tour packages if you prefer to combine rooms, meals and transport.

Before paying for accommodation

Confirm the property's name and address, room type, dates, total price, payment terms and facilities in writing. Check official Spiti road updates before travelling towards Spiti. If travelling from another Indian city, consult our Pan-India joining guide.

Frequently asked questions

Can I enquire for hotels without booking a tour?

Yes. Share travel dates, overnight towns, number of guests and room requirements. Individual property availability must be confirmed.

Are heated rooms available in winter Spiti?

Some operating properties may offer heating or electric blankets. Confirm the specific facilities, power arrangements and availability before booking.

Should I stay in Kaza or Tabo?

Kaza suits local sightseeing, while Tabo may suit an overnight stop on the approach. Choose according to your route, pace and acclimatisation needs.
