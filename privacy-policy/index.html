Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Himalayan Odyssey Expedition respects the privacy of people who visit this website or contact us about a Himalayan trip. This page explains what information is handled when you send a travel enquiry and how you can contact us about it.

In short: The website enquiry form asks for your name, mobile number, group size, destination and travel timing. The form is designed to save the enquiry in our customer and lead records and then open WhatsApp so you can review and send a message to our team.

Information you provide

If you use the trip enquiry form, we process the details you enter, including your name, phone number, number of travellers, destination, travel month and any travel dates you provide. If you contact us directly, we may also receive the information you choose to include in your WhatsApp message, phone call or Instagram message.

How we use enquiry information

  • To respond to questions about tours, stays, routes and availability.
  • To prepare or follow up on a trip proposal or booking enquiry.
  • To maintain customer and lead records so our team can manage your request.
  • To communicate important information about an enquiry or trip you ask us to arrange.

Where information is handled

The website uses Supabase as its hosted database service for customer and lead records. When you choose to send a WhatsApp message, you are using WhatsApp and its own service and privacy terms. We do not control the privacy practices of third-party platforms.

Website analytics

Optional Google Analytics 4 event tracking has been prepared in the website code but remains inactive until a valid measurement ID is configured. If analytics is enabled, this notice should be reviewed and updated to describe the analytics data and any consent controls used before it is activated.

Sharing and security

We do not sell personal contact details. Information may be handled by service providers needed to operate the website and communicate with you. We use reasonable safeguards, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

How long we keep information

We keep enquiry details for as long as reasonably needed to respond, manage a potential or confirmed trip, maintain business records and address follow-up questions. You can contact us to ask about the information associated with your enquiry.

Your choices and requests

You may ask us to correct inaccurate contact details or discuss deletion of enquiry information, subject to records we need to retain for legitimate business or legal reasons. Please include enough context for us to locate the enquiry, but do not send passwords, payment card details or other sensitive credentials.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact Himalayan Odyssey Expedition, Narkanda, Himachal Pradesh, by phone or WhatsApp at +91 98169 29669, or through WhatsApp.

This policy is a plain-language website notice and is not legal advice. It should be reviewed against the business's actual data handling and applicable legal requirements.
