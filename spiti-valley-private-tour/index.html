Private and customised Spiti Valley tour packages

Travel with your own party rather than joining a fixed group departure. Himalayan Odyssey Expedition can prepare a route-specific enquiry for a private Spiti Valley package with a dedicated vehicle, overnight stays and sightseeing.

Customise your Spiti itinerary

Tell us your available days, starting point and must-visit places. An example eight-day circuit from Chandigarh uses Narkanda, Sangla, Tabo, two nights in Kaza, Kalpa and Shimla, with the final return to Chandigarh. The sequence can be discussed for your dates; keep realistic road days and rest at altitude.

Private tour from Chandigarh, Delhi or Shimla

Confirm your actual pickup and final drop before arranging flights or trains. Chandigarh and Shimla joining options can be discussed; Delhi pickup requires a separate confirmation and may change the duration and cost. Travellers from elsewhere in India can use our joining guide.

Private Spiti Valley tour from Shimla or Narkanda

Already staying in Shimla or Narkanda? Request a custom round trip via Kinnaur, Tabo and Kaza, with the final halt and drop agreed separately. Compared with a Chandigarh start, this may change the first and last transfer days; the rest of the itinerary still needs realistic travel time and acclimatisation. A summer Manali exit is a separate seasonal option, not a winter route.

Share the pickup address, number of travellers, available nights, room requirements and return deadline. Request a group-total quote and the corresponding per-person cost for your actual party size. Compare winter group and private vehicle options, Narkanda stay arrangements and transport-only services.

Choose the vehicle and accommodation

Share passenger count and luggage. Discuss a suitable cab, SUV or Tempo Traveller with the operator and confirm road suitability. Select the room type and occupancy for each night; breakfast and dinner may be included if specified. For transport alone, see our private cab enquiries.

Private Spiti Valley SUV tour for families, couples and small groups

For a private road trip, tell us whether you prefer a dedicated SUV, a smaller cab or a Tempo Traveller for your party. A winter 4x4 request is assessed separately for actual road conditions and vehicle availability; it is not a standard inclusion. Ask for a quote that identifies vehicle type, driver charges, overnight rooms, meals and any separate local sightseeing transfer.

Compare Spiti taxi and SUV hire, family holiday packages and couple trips according to your pace and room requirements.

Summer or winter private Spiti trip

Winter itineraries use the Kinnaur approach and need flexibility for snow, heating and closures. In summer, a wider circuit with Chandratal and a Manali exit can be considered only when official access allows. See winter trips and Chandratal planning.

Private Spiti package price and inclusions

The price depends on vehicle, dates, passenger count, accommodation, route, meals and any local transfers. Ask for a total party price and per-person equivalent with taxes, exclusions, cancellation terms and extra-day charges. A customised trip does not have a universal fixed tariff.

Confirm your route and booking before travel

Check official district road status, request a written itinerary and quotation, and confirm pickup, accommodation, vehicle, meals and cancellation terms. Actual availability and road access must be checked for your dates. Compare all Spiti Valley tour options before deciding.

Frequently asked questions

Can I customise a Spiti tour for my own dates?

Yes, send your dates, group size, route preferences and pickup point. The itinerary and availability must be confirmed.

Will we have our own vehicle?

Request a dedicated vehicle in the quotation. Vehicle type, local transfers and driver arrangements must be agreed before booking.

Can I choose hotels and sightseeing stops?

Yes, share your preferences. The final plan depends on available rooms, road access, travel time and acclimatisation.
