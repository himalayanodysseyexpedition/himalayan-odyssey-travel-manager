Winter Spiti Hotels and Homestays: 2027 Stay Checklist

A room listing cannot tell you how a high-altitude property operates in freezing conditions. Before booking winter stays in Tabo or Kaza, verify which facilities will actually be available on your dates.

By Himalayan Odyssey Expedition · Published 26 November 2026 · Planning guide for 2027

What makes a winter stay suitable in Spiti?

Look for a property that confirms its winter opening dates, practical heating arrangements, drinking water, toilet access, meals and a reachable approach road. Ask for these details in writing rather than assuming summer facilities operate all year. Winter conditions in Spiti can be severe, and local access can change quickly. See Himachal Tourism's Spiti overview for seasonal context.

Tabo or Kaza: how to plan the overnight stops

Tabo is a useful stop on the approach from Kinnaur, while Kaza is a common base for Spiti sightseeing. A night in Tabo can break up the drive before continuing to Kaza. That does not remove altitude-related risks; travellers still need a gradual itinerary and appropriate medical advice. Village excursions should remain conditional on local access. Choose overnight locations according to the actual route rather than just a photograph of a hotel.

Eight practical questions for winter accommodation

  1. Are you open for my entire stay? Ask whether the property is operating on the exact dates and whether the kitchen will be staffed.
  2. How is the bedroom heated? Confirm whether a heated room, electric blanket or other arrangement is available and what happens during a power cut. Ask whether it is included in the quoted price.
  3. Is the common room heated? If a property uses a bukhari or another combustion heater, confirm proper ventilation, safe operation and carbon monoxide precautions. Do not use unvented combustion appliances in sleeping rooms.
  4. What is the bathroom arrangement? Ask if running water may freeze, whether there is stored water, and if hot water and a functional toilet are available. Confirm whether the bathroom is private or shared.
  5. Can the kitchen serve meals? Check breakfast and dinner timing, dietary requirements and packed food options for early departures. Do not count on every nearby restaurant being open.
  6. What happens if electricity fails? Ask about backup lighting, charging and the availability of alternative warmth. Carry a charged power bank and warm sleeping layers.
  7. Can the vehicle reach the property? Check parking, walking distance from the road, snow clearance and luggage access. A short distance on a map can still be difficult on ice.
  8. What if weather changes the arrival date? Ask about moving the booking, extra nights and cancellation or refund terms. Get the contact number for the person coordinating winter arrivals.

Hotel versus homestay

Compare the individual property, not the label. A smaller homestay may offer a communal dining space, while a hotel may offer more room categories. Neither guarantees heating, hot water or continuous power. Ask for the same checklist and room-sharing information from both. What should you carry overnight?

Keep thermals, insulated outerwear, dry socks, gloves, a torch, power bank, water bottle and essential personal medication in an accessible bag rather than at the bottom of the vehicle luggage. Bring enough warm layers to remain comfortable if electricity is interrupted. Discuss individual health considerations with a clinician before travelling to high altitude; see CDC altitude guidance.

Check the road before you confirm

Do not treat a room reservation as confirmation that the road will remain open. Use the district road-status page and local official advisories, then coordinate the drive and arrival with your operator. Keep a flexible alternative overnight stop and enough time before your onward transport.

Request a winter accommodation quote

Send your arrival date, overnight towns, guest count, number of rooms, meal requirements and heating preferences. Ask which facilities are confirmed at each property and whether a weather-related change would incur extra charges. A written confirmation is more useful than a generic promise of a premium stay.

Frequently asked questions

Are electric blankets guaranteed in every winter Spiti hotel?

No. Confirm whether they are provided for your room and what happens if the power fails.

Is hot running water always available in Tabo and Kaza?

No. Water systems can be affected by freezing conditions; ask about the property's alternative arrangements.

Can I book a stay without confirming the road?

A reservation does not guarantee road access. Check official advisories and coordinate any changes with the property and driver.
