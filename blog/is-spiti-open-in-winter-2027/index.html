Is Spiti Valley Open in Winter 2027? Routes and Road Conditions

The short answer is that Spiti can be reached in winter in many seasons, but access is never guaranteed on a particular date. The route from Shimla through Kinnaur is the usual winter approach to Kaza. The Manali route over Kunzum Pass generally closes under snow. Is Spiti open throughout winter?

Spiti is a living high-altitude region, not a seasonal resort with one fixed opening date. The main road approach through Kinnaur may remain usable for much of the year, but snowfall, ice, landslides, rockfall and road maintenance can temporarily stop traffic. A route described as almost all-weather should never be interpreted as an assurance that a vehicle will pass on a chosen day.

The Lahaul and Spiti district administration's How to Reach page identifies Shimla to Kaza via Kinnaur as an almost all-weather road connection subject to prevailing weather and road conditions. It also notes that the Keylong–Kaza route via Kunzum Pass generally remains closed during winter, with opening dependent on snow conditions.

Which route is used in winter?

The usual route is Shimla → Narkanda → Rampur → Reckong Peo/Kalpa → Nako → Sumdo → Tabo → Kaza. The exact overnight stops depend on daylight, road status, driver assessment and the time of year. Some travellers break the journey around Kinnaur rather than attempting a long continuous drive.

In winter, do not build an itinerary around a Manali-to-Kaza crossing. The road via Atal Tunnel, Gramphu, Batal and Kunzum Pass is a different seasonal route. Atal Tunnel being open does not mean Kunzum Pass is open.

What can cause a closure?

  • Fresh snowfall and accumulated snow on exposed sections.
  • Black ice, particularly in shaded stretches and early mornings.
  • Rockfall or landslides along steep valley roads.
  • Road clearance, repair work or restrictions issued by local authorities.
  • Heavy rain or sudden weather changes during shoulder seasons.

Closures can affect a small stretch rather than the entire highway. That is why a broad statement that “Spiti is open” is not enough to decide whether your specific journey is safe.

How to check before departure

Check district administration and police advisories, local road updates and weather forecasts close to the travel date. Confirm again with your driver or local accommodation before starting each leg. If authorities advise against travel, postpone the drive. Do not rely only on old social media posts, map travel times or a route status from a different week.

Can you visit villages around Kaza?

Not automatically. The main road to Kaza and the smaller roads to nearby villages can have different conditions. A village excursion should be decided on the same day after checking road access, snow and local advice. Avoid promising yourself a fixed checklist of high villages in deep winter.

How should you plan your dates?

Choose dates with flexibility rather than scheduling every stop tightly. Keep buffer days, confirm winter accommodation, ask about heating and meals, and carry suitable clothing. Travellers should be prepared for limited services and intermittent network connectivity. If your schedule cannot absorb a delay, a winter trip may not be the right choice.

Frequently asked questions

Is Spiti open in December?

The Kinnaur-side road may remain usable, but snowfall and other disruptions can cause temporary closures. Check the latest official status for your exact dates.

Is the Manali–Kaza road open in January?

Kunzum Pass generally remains closed during winter. Do not assume the Manali route is available.

Does an open road mean all Spiti villages are accessible?

No. Local village roads can be blocked even when the main road to Kaza is open.
