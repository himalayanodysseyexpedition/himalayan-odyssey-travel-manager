By Himalayan Odyssey Expedition · Updated 20 November 2026 · Planning guide for 2027

Winter and summer offer very different experiences in Spiti. Summer is usually easier for wider route access and village exploration; winter brings snow-covered landscapes, quieter settlements and more demanding travel conditions. The better season depends on your priorities, not on a single universal answer.

Summer Spiti: more route options

From late spring through early autumn, road access generally improves as snow clears from high passes. When the Manali–Kunzum route is officially open, travellers may plan a circuit that enters through Kinnaur and exits towards Manali, or the reverse if current conditions allow. The exact opening window changes from year to year, and the pass can close again after fresh snowfall.

Summer is usually better for travellers who want a broader route, more local excursions and a higher chance of finding seasonal services operating. It is also the busier travel period, so accommodation and vehicles should be booked after checking the actual operating dates.

Winter Spiti: snow and a slower pace

Winter is for travellers who specifically want a cold-desert snow experience and are prepared for disruption. The usual road approach to Kaza is through Shimla and Kinnaur. The Keylong–Kaza road over Kunzum Pass generally remains closed during winter. Local village access can be restricted, accommodation choices may be fewer and temperatures can become severe.

Himachal Tourism notes that Spiti remains cold through the year and that winter temperatures can fall to around minus 20°C in some places. Read its destination information and prepare for cold conditions.

Which season is better for first-time visitors?

For many first-time visitors, the warmer season is easier to plan because more route options and services may be available. It still requires acclimatisation, sensible driving days and current road checks. If your main goal is snow and you can accept limited services and delays, winter can be rewarding, but it should not be treated as a standard sightseeing holiday.

What about spring and autumn?

Shoulder seasons can offer fewer crowds and changing landscapes, but they can also bring uncertain weather and transitions in road access. A pass may not yet have opened in spring or may close earlier than expected in autumn. Confirm the route for your exact dates rather than relying on a generic seasonal calendar.

How should you choose?

  • Choose summer if you want the possibility of a wider circuit and more village stops.
  • Choose winter if snow is your priority and you are prepared for cold, fewer services and route changes.
  • Choose a shoulder season only if you can be flexible about what remains accessible.
  • For any season, keep buffer time and do not drive into a closed or restricted road.

Frequently asked questions

Is summer better than winter for Spiti?

Summer generally offers more route options and services. Winter is colder, quieter and more weather-dependent; the better season depends on your travel priorities.

Can I complete the Spiti circuit in winter?

Do not plan a circuit over Kunzum Pass in winter. The pass generally closes due to snow.

When does Kunzum Pass open?

There is no guaranteed annual opening date. Opening depends on snow clearance, road condition and official decisions.
