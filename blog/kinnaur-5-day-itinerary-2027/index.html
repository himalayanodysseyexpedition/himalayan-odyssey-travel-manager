Kinnaur 5-Day Itinerary 2027: Kalpa, Sangla and Chitkul

A practical five-day Kinnaur route for travellers who want to explore the Sutlej valley, Kalpa and the Baspa valley without rushing every stop. Use it as a planning framework, then adjust it to road conditions and your starting point.

By Himalayan Odyssey Expedition · Published 20 November 2026 · Planning guide for 2027

Kinnaur is best explored at a pace that leaves room for mountain roads, village visits and changing weather. A five-day trip can cover the main highlights around Kalpa, Sangla and Chitkul, but it is a compact itinerary rather than a guarantee that every stop will be accessible on every date. This 2027 guide lays out a sensible route from Shimla and explains where to keep flexibility. Compare our Kinnaur tour package from Chandigarh or Shimla for a customised itinerary and quotation.

At a glance: the five-day route

  1. Day 1: Shimla or Narkanda to Sarahan or the Rampur area; overnight halt.
  2. Day 2: Continue via the Sutlej valley to Sangla; explore the village if time and road conditions allow.
  3. Day 3: Sangla, Rakcham and Chitkul excursion; return to Sangla for the night.
  4. Day 4: Drive to Kalpa via Karcham and Reckong Peo; spend the evening in Kalpa.
  5. Day 5: Kalpa viewpoint and local visit, then begin the return journey or extend the trip.

If you are starting from Chandigarh or Delhi, add a night before this route or extend the trip to six or seven days. Long mountain drives should not be squeezed into a late arrival.

Day 1: break the journey towards Kinnaur

Start from Shimla and follow the Hindustan-Tibet road towards Narkanda, Rampur and Sarahan. The best overnight stop depends on departure time, traffic and the condition of the road. Sarahan can be a worthwhile detour for travellers who want to include Bhimakali Temple; otherwise, choose a practical halt that keeps the next day's drive manageable.

Day 2: reach Sangla in the Baspa valley

Continue along the Sutlej valley and turn towards the Baspa valley near Karcham. Sangla is a useful base for exploring the valley. Allow time for check-in and a relaxed village walk rather than planning several distant stops on the same afternoon. Himachal Tourism's official Kinnaur guide describes Sangla, Kamru and Chitkul and provides regional travel context.

Day 3: Sangla, Rakcham and Chitkul

Keep this day flexible for the road towards Chitkul. The route is a mountain valley road, and the time required depends on traffic, weather and local restrictions. Visit village areas respectfully, follow instructions at checkpoints and do not proceed beyond permitted civilian limits. Return to Sangla for the night unless a locally verified alternative has been arranged.

Day 4: Kalpa and the Kinner Kailash views

Return to the main highway and continue via Reckong Peo to Kalpa. Kalpa sits above the district headquarters and is known for views towards the Kinner Kailash range when the weather is clear. Keep the afternoon light: road travel, check-in and a short local walk are usually more realistic than a packed sightseeing list.

Day 5: local time and return planning

Use the morning for a viewpoint or a short village visit, then begin the return. If your onward journey is to Chandigarh or Delhi, consider adding a stopover rather than attempting an exhausting single push after several days on mountain roads.

When should you visit Kinnaur?

Spring through autumn is the broad planning window for many visitors, while winter brings freezing temperatures and a higher chance of road disruption. The official tourism guide notes that temperatures can fall below freezing in December and January. The best month for your group depends on whether you prioritise green valleys, apple season, clear mountain views or snow. Do not book on the assumption that a specific snowfall event or road opening is guaranteed.

Transport, stays and budget factors

For a private trip, compare the cost of a dedicated vehicle for the full route with locally arranged transfers. A vehicle and driver familiar with the region can help manage narrow roads and changing conditions. Accommodation costs vary by village, travel dates, room type and meal plan. Frequently asked questions

Can I cover Kalpa, Sangla and Chitkul in five days?

Yes, five days can cover the main places from a Shimla-side start, but it is a compact plan. Add time if starting from Delhi or Chandigarh or if you want a slower pace.

Is Chitkul always accessible?

No. Access can change with snowfall, rain, road work and local instructions. Confirm the latest conditions before travelling.

Is a 4x4 compulsory for Kinnaur?

Not for every trip or road. Vehicle choice should depend on the exact route, season and current conditions; ask a local operator to assess the itinerary.

What is a sensible starting point?

Shimla is a practical road gateway for a Kinnaur itinerary. Travellers coming from farther away should usually add a stopover.
